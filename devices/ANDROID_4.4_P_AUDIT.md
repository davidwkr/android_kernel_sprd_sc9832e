# Android common kernel 4.4-p audit

Input for the next step (cherry-picking `aosp/android-4.4-p` fixes in small reviewed batches). Analysis only: nothing was picked. Generated by `tools/ack_audit.py` (run `python3 -I tools/ack_audit.py OUT.tsv --config <dm63 .config>`), then classified and curated by hand.

## Method

1. Candidates: the 1480 non-merge commits of `aosp/android-4.4-p` (not on its stable parent) whose subject starts with `ANDROID:`, `UPSTREAM:`, `BACKPORT:`, `FROMLIST:`, `FROMGIT:` (also inside `Revert "..."`).
2. Presence in this tree, first match wins:
   - `PATCHID`: `git patch-id --stable` equals one of our commits since the vendor base.
   - `CHERRYSHA`: its `cherry picked from commit` sha appears in one of our commit messages.
   - `SUBJECT`: normalized subject (prefix, case and punctuation removed) equals one of our subjects or a bullet line of a `stable: Linux 4.4.N` message.
   - `NOFILES`: none of the touched files exist in our tree.
   - `REVAPPLIED`: the diff reverse-applies cleanly to HEAD (temporary index only).
   - `ADDEDLINES`: at least 80% of the added lines (2 or more, length 12 or more) already exist in the touched files at HEAD. Heuristic: catches the vendor tree and stable merges, which have no shared history with aosp.
   - otherwise `MISSING`.
3. Conflict estimate for MISSING: `git apply --check` on a temporary index: clean = low, clean with `-C1` = med, fails = high.
4. Build gate: dm63 config (`scripts/watch_defconfig.sh` output, `make O=<tmp> dm63_defconfig`, no build); each `.c`/`.S` file is resolved through the `obj-$(CONFIG_*)` chain of the Makefiles. `y` built, `n` not built, `?` unresolved (headers, conditional objects).
5. Class of MISSING: (a) CVE, security wording, or sensitive subsystem plus fix wording; (b) features/infra, or code not built; (c) other bugfix in built code; (d) not applicable (docs/config/other arch/absent files/reverts). (a) was pruned by hand (9 non-fixes moved to b).
6. `era`: `pre147` = reachable from aosp "Merge 4.4.147" (the vendor base kernel version), `post147` = later.

Caveats:
- PATCHID and CHERRYSHA find nothing: our history has no shared ancestry with aosp and stable merges are one commit per release. Presence rests on the content heuristics, so a MISSING can still be present in another form (the vendor binder, f2fs and netfilter code are rewritten or newer than aosp). Verify with a grep before picking, especially every `pre147` entry: most FROMLIST/ANDROID binder items there were superseded by the final upstream binder code the vendor tree already has (fine-grained locking is present).
- `post147` entries are the better candidates: they cannot be in the vendor base. The 4.4.148-302 merges already cover the upstream parts of many `UPSTREAM:` commits.
- Pick series together (for example binder `proc->files`: FROMLIST, revert, UPSTREAM final), and take the final upstream form only.
- HID sony (many `c` entries) is built in dm63 but only reachable through physical USB/Bluetooth HID; low priority.
